Bundled source extensions
=========================

The APK files in this folder are unmodified builds of community extensions for Mihon/Tachiyomi, published by the
Keiyoushi project (https://github.com/keiyoushi/extensions, signing certificate SHA-256
9add655a78e96c4ec7a53ef89dccb557cb5d767489fac5e785d671a5a75d4da2) under the Apache License 2.0
(https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

Only extensions for official platforms and open catalogs are bundled:
  * MangaDex            (eu.kanade.tachiyomi.extension.all.mangadex, 1.6.1)
  * MANGA Plus          (eu.kanade.tachiyomi.extension.all.mangaplus, 1.6.68)
  * Webtoons.com        (eu.kanade.tachiyomi.extension.all.webtoons, 1.6.3)
  * GlobalComix         (eu.kanade.tachiyomi.extension.all.globalcomix, 1.6.2)
  * AComics             (eu.kanade.tachiyomi.extension.ru.acomics, 1.6.10)

Yomu loads them through its Tachiyomi compatibility layer; they can be removed, and updated from any store signed with
the same key.
