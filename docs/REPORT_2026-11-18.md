## What changed

### Reading (M03a/M03b)

- `domain/reader/ReaderWindow.kt`: bounded chapter window (`current ± 1`), chapter/page anchors and the flat page
  layout over loaded chapters; tests cover edges, shifts, missing pages and a group switch.
- `feature/reader-manga/SessionPageSource.kt`: one flat `PageSource` over 2–3 chapters, chapter identity for every
  page, transfer of retained chapters and release of the far one; tests cover mapping, lifetime and transfer.
- `MangaReaderView`: chapter boundary slots between loaded chapters, chapter title banner, local page chip, pager
  mapping that keeps spreads/split halves per chapter, and preloading that crosses a boundary without a read mark.
- `ReaderViewModel`: opening builds the window; `openAdjacentChapter` turns inside the loaded window without
  `UiState.Loading`, re-centres after the turn, saves the chapter being left, keeps the current page on a neighbour
  failure and reports it.
- `WindowHtmlDocument` + `openTextWindow`: online novels hold only the current ±1 chapter with a section↔chapter
  bridge and an offset anchor; the window re-centres without a loading screen.

### Audio (A01/A02/A03)

- `AudioOfflinePlan` (ALL/NEXT_N/SELECTED) with per-book persistence, track-level states, size estimate with
  unknown lengths, free-space guard, selective removal that keeps the manifest, stream URI and position.
- `AudioSettings` additions with migration: rewind `OFF/ADAPTIVE/CUSTOM`, offline plan, metadata overrides,
  gain opt-in. `rewindAfter` keeps adaptive behaviour for old books and ignores a sub-3 s blip.
- One command owner: the screen's skip buttons, notification, headset and Bluetooth all go through the service's
  `ForwardingPlayer` (`CMD_SKIP`); rewind settings travel through `CMD_REWIND`.
- ReplayGain reader (ID3 TXXX, Vorbis), peak-aware factor that cannot clip, opt-in gain, sleep fade multiplied by
  the gain.
- Metadata overrides in `MediaMetadata` (narrator/series/description, total duration), book-details sheet.
- Linked text↔audio editions (`LinkedEditions`), audio widget (`AudioWidgetContract`, `AudioWidgetProvider`,
  one service command per button, no autoplay after reboot), 48 dp transport buttons.

### Data and sync (MG01/B01/B02/B03)

- Migration plan/apply: pure `MigrationMatcher` (exact/likely/review/missing), preview without writes, revision
  checks, category choice, one transaction, journal written before commit, reversible undo, idempotent second apply,
  batch with independent results; UI dialog with counts and category checkboxes.
- Backup v2: category manifest, preview counts, merge/replace choice, stable-identity merge and numeric-id remap of
  dependent records, archive version check, entry/byte limits, staged files removed on rollback; settings restored
  only when the category is chosen.
- Sync: schema 18 `books.metaUpdatedAt`; per-book settings/rating/favourite merge newest-wins; podcast
  subscriptions by feed URL with `s|` tombstones and book-hash remapping; `SyncCodec` preserves unknown top-level
  categories from a newer client.
- `SyncWizard` pure state machine, recovery kinds and one `SyncStatusSummary` in Settings.

### Library, reader settings, words, tasks (L05/N02/N04/N05)

- `LibraryQuerySpec` persists the last query; `SavedQueryShelf` chips restore, apply and delete smart shelves.
- `ReaderOptions` registry by engine/mode, used by the quick panel; TTS voice sample; PDF crop preview with

- Word list filter by book; the existing word→book+locator jump remains.
- `LongTask`/`TaskRegistry`: progress, cancel request/acknowledgement, partial errors, output, “cancelled is never
